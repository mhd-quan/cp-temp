# v1.1.1 — A faster first screen on phones, a quieter scroll on desktops

Performance release following a WebPageTest report of the live homepage on a
phone and feedback that scrolling was less smooth on desktops than on phones.
No design changes. Details, measurements and limits are in
[`docs/redesign/11-v1-1-1.md`](../redesign/11-v1-1-1.md) (Vietnamese).

## Changes

- **The hand.** When the page scrolls under a resting mouse, browsers hover
  whatever slides beneath it without the hand moving. At every pause of a
  reading scroll a lamp was lit, a title underlined, a book lifted and an
  essay prerendered, and all of it was undone as the next scroll began. The
  root now carries `data-resting` from a scroll under a mouse until the mouse
  moves (`src/lib/editionHand.ts`): every `:hover` rule of the edition waits
  under `:where(:root:not([data-resting]))` (specificity unchanged, focus
  never gated), and the lamp, the series decks, the reader's notes and link
  speculation wait for the hand. The scroll shield of v1.1 stays.
- **Speculation on intent.** The static "moderate" prerender rule and Astro's
  hover prefetch are replaced by `src/lib/editionSpeculation.ts`: a hand that
  has moved and rests on a link (200 ms for an essay, 80 ms for any other
  page), a keyboard focus or a press prerenders an essay or prefetches a page;
  at most two essays stay prerendered. Astro's prefetch script is gone from
  every page.
- **Prints.** The first print of a page (the homepage cover, an essay's
  cover) is printed inked by the server: never hidden, never faded in. Other
  prints fade in only if they are in view when their image arrives; one that
  arrives out of sight is laid at once (`data-inked="quiet"`). At night the
  paper is dimmed (`color-mix(in srgb, …)`) instead of every plate carrying
  `filter: brightness(0.86)`; screenshots differ by at most 1/255.
- **Fonts.** `scripts/instance-fonts.mjs` pins the axes the edition never
  varies: Mona Sans at its one width, 112% (Latin 98 → 40 KB, Vietnamese
  14 → 7 KB), Libre Bodoni at its one weight, 500 (26 → 16 KB). Mona Sans
  reserves its name under the OFL, so the instance is named Pensees Sans. The
  Vietnamese subset of the sans is preloaded (its capitals are on the first
  screen). Fallback stacks name only faces that can stand in: every missing
  name was a system font lookup during the first layout.
- **Layout.** The shelf is measured in its first `ResizeObserver` callback
  instead of forcing a layout before the first frame.
- **Tools.** `scripts/scroll-bench.mjs` measures a desktop reading scroll
  with the mouse on or off the page; `scripts/hand-browser.mjs` joins the
  browser suite.

## Measurements

Headless Chromium 141, no GPU; stand-in images (see limits).

| Phone, first visit (390×663 @3x, 4× CPU, 170 ms RTT; median of 11) | v1.1.0 | v1.1.1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LCP | 928 ms | 592 ms |
| Layout, total to load | 393 ms | 251 ms |
| Style, total to load | 118 ms | 91 ms |
| Phone, repeat visit: LCP (median of 7) | 1016 ms | 704 ms |

| Desktop reading scroll, mouse resting on the page (1440×900; mean of 3) | v1.1.0 | v1.1.1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Style / paint / layerize | 79 / 125 / 127 ms | 25 / 38 / 31 ms |
| Raster | 401 ms | 185 ms (as with the mouse off the page) |
| Lamps lit, pages fetched unasked | 13, 3 | 0, 0 |

## Outside the repository

Two scripts in the report are injected by Cloudflare at the edge: JavaScript
Detections (`/cdn-cgi/challenge-platform/…/jsd/main.js`, 94 ms of the 116 ms
TBT) and the Web Analytics beacon. Both are dashboard settings.

## Verification

`astro check` (0 errors, 0 warnings), `biome check` (no errors), `npm test`
(91/91) and the browser suite (`npm run test:browser`, now including the hand
check) pass. Full-page screenshots of the homepage, an essay and the books
page at 390 and 1440 px, light and dark, match v1.1.0 in size to the pixel;
they differ only in text antialiasing and by 1/255 on night plates. Not yet
checked on a physical iPhone or Android phone.
