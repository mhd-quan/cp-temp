# v1.1.0 — One type scale, a continuous homepage, a lighter scroll

Design and performance release following review of v1.0.0 in production.
Details, measurements and limits are in
[`docs/redesign/10-v1-1.md`](../redesign/10-v1-1.md) (Vietnamese).

## Changes

- **Type scale.** Every size on an edition page is a step of one ladder of
  role tokens (`foundation.css`). Sans capitals drop to 11/12px with 0.08em
  tracking; rubrics (topic tags) keep the size of a label and rely on their ink.
  Titles are about 10% smaller; module heads 68 → 44px.
- **Mobile masthead.** "The" sits small in the top row beside the menu button
  and "Cochinchine Pensées" spans the measure (10.6cqi). The truncated double
  rule is gone on phones. The bar and the menu carry the same lockup, smaller
  (25px), above every feed title.
- **Homepage rhythm.** Module heads open with a short ink mark instead of a
  full-width rule; module spacing 72–144 → 48–96px; the doubled space under
  the review shelf is removed.
- **Bar.** The bar's paper runs from solid at its top edge to 80% at its
  foot, over the same blur. Safari 26 paints the status bar above a top-edge
  bar in the page's paper (other phones use `theme-color`, now the paper
  too), so band and bar join with no edge. A first attempt (an invisible bar
  frame with the material on `::before`) lost the blur on iPhone and was
  withdrawn. Where scroll-driven animations exist the floating bar is tied to
  the masthead's exit and can no longer lag behind a fast scroll to the top.
- **Reader papers.** Colour mixes move from oklch to oklab: near-grey papers
  (Rêu, Sương) lost their hue in oklch and the bar and sheets printed pink.
  `theme-color` follows the actual paper.
- **Scrolling.** A transparent shield covers the page while it scrolls under a
  resting mouse, so hover, the reading lamp and book lifts no longer restyle,
  repaint and re-raster the page mid-scroll (paint −50%, raster −37%).
- **Images.** Series deck prints declare their drawn width (800px files on a
  3x phone instead of 1200px); the visible series loads early, other series'
  top prints load at low priority, a tab fetches its series on intent; lazy
  images on edition pages start loading 1.5 screens ahead.
- **Motion.** The resume prompt, quotation tools, copy status, series tab
  switch and publication history now leave as well as arrive.
- **Reader settings.** The typeface picker shows only "Aa" in each face; names
  are for screen readers, and the Lexend note is removed.

## Verification

`astro check` (0 errors), `biome check`, `npm test` (88/88) and the browser
suite (`npm run test:browser`) pass. The bar's join with the status bar is
simulated in Chromium; it has not yet been checked on a physical iPhone.
